Photographs that work within your business: on a website, in an advertisement, or as part of a larger campaign.

Keith A. Godfrey combines commercial photography with a background in graphic design, advertising, and print production. He considers the finished use of an image alongside the subject itself, from the first project discussion through delivery.

Questions before you book

What should I include in a product photography inquiry?

Describe the products or subjects, how many images or views you have in mind, the intended use, and your schedule. Include any layout or brand requirements that will affect the photographs.

Can the photography be planned around a website or campaign?

Yes. Keith’s background includes graphic design, advertising, and print production. Discuss the planned placements so the photography can be considered within the wider project.

What files are delivered?

Deliverables are high-resolution RAW image files. Discuss any additional format or size requirements for your website, advertisement, or print production before arranging the shoot.

Does the work include editing?

Basic editing is available. Explain the finishing you need during the project discussion.

Can delivery be planned around my deadline?

Turnaround depends on your schedule and the project. Give Keith your deadline at the start so the timing can be discussed along with the photography arrangements.

What rights will I have to the photographs?

You retain full rights to your images.
