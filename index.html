02 / The perspective

A considered
point of view.

Keith A. Godfrey is a photographer, photo editor, and graphic designer with four decades behind the camera.

His work runs from directed studio portraits and commercial product shoots to landscapes, wildlife, and close-up studies of rust and weathered wood. Every image starts the same way: he sees the photograph before he presses the shutter, then uses light, composition, and careful finishing to bring it to life.
