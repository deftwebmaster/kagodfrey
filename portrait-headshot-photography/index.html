The approach

A portrait begins before the camera.

A business headshot and a personal portrait have different jobs to do. Keith’s approach begins with understanding yours. His background in commercial photography and graphic design helps him consider how a photograph will sit within a profile, website, printed piece, or wider visual identity.

Questions before you book

How do I arrange a portrait or headshot session?

Call Keith or send a message describing what you need. Session arrangements are discussed with him first, including the people involved, the intended use, and the setup.

What should I share before a headshot session?

Tell Keith where the headshot will appear, any image requirements you have been given, and the deadline you are working toward. You can also bring examples of the look you have in mind.

What image files will I receive?

Deliverables are high-resolution RAW image files. If your profile, website, or printer needs a particular format or size, discuss that requirement with Keith before the session.

Is editing available?

Basic editing is available. Discuss the finishing you need with Keith when planning the session.

How long will delivery take?

Turnaround depends on your schedule and the project. Share your deadline when you get in touch so the timing can be discussed before the session.

Who retains the rights to the photographs?

You retain full rights to your images.
